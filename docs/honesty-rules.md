# Honesty rules (non-negotiable)

1. Every number, price, date, limit and claim comes from a primary source: the official site, docs, pricing page
   or README. Write the source and the date checked in the episode file header.
2. If a fact can change (free plans, offers, limits), put "checked on <date>" on screen or in the caption.
3. If the recorded voice is out of date, keep the voice, and show the correction on screen as a dated "update".
4. No income promises. Money reels say what the money depends on (approval, budgets, views) and "results vary".
5. Real numbers only: no invented views, sales, comments, testimonials, screenshots or DMs. Friends' test
   purchases are never counted as sales.
6. Paid things are named as paid ("Claude Code needs a paid Claude plan", "credits", "auto-renews").
7. Say what we have not tested ourselves.
8. Third-party projects: name the licence and say "community project, not made by <company>" when true.
9. Copyright: only use clips, art and logos we have the right to use. For clipping topics, say "only clip
   creators who allow it".
10. Instagram DMs are restricted: never show or promise automatic DM delivery.
