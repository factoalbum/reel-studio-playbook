# Scripts and hooks

## Shape (30 to 95 seconds)

1. **Hook (0 to 3 s)**: a trend, a date or a money/free surprise in one line. Examples of the shape:
   "19 November ko X aa raha hai, aur us din log Y karke paise banayenge." / "Claude Code free mein?"
2. **What it is (3 to 10 s)**: one plain sentence.
3. **The real number or the catch (10 to 18 s)**: the fact that makes it believable.
4. **3 to 5 steps**: each step short, each gets its own picture.
5. **Honest note**: limits, cost, risk.
6. **Call to action**: "Comment karo KEYWORD, ya link in bio pe KEYWORD type karo." Plus one question for comments.

## Language

Roman Hinglish, spoken style, short sentences. English for tool names. Lowercase is fine in captions, not in the script.

## What works (from our numbers)

- Free working thing + a comment keyword, riding a trend people already search for, beats product ads.
- Our best Instagram reel (FreeLLMAPI, 17 Nov 2026): 1,287 views, 32 saves and 20 shares in the first hours.
- Pure self-promotion reels flop. Teach first.

## Keywords

Each reel gets one new keyword (letters and numbers). It must open a free page on internetoolbox.com/links.
The site accepts the word with or without a final S and one small typo.
